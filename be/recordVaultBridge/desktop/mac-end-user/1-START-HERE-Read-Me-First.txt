========================================================
  usbBridgeV3 for Mac — START HERE
  (After you clicked “Download usbbridge zip” on the website)
========================================================

You do NOT need Terminal.
Use only Finder double-clicks and normal Mac buttons.


BEFORE THE 4 STEPS — Unzip the download
---------------------------------------
  1. Open Finder → Downloads
  2. Double-click:  usbBridgeV3-mac.zip
     (This creates a folder named usbBridgeV3-mac)
  3. Open that folder. You should see:
       • 1-START-HERE-Read-Me-First.txt   (this file)
       • 2-Open-Privacy-Settings.webloc
       • usbBridgeV3.app

  If you still see “Open usbBridgeV3.command” and NOT the
  two files above, delete the old folder and download again
  from the website (you have an old zip).


Why Mac shows a warning
-----------------------
This app is not yet signed with an Apple certificate.
macOS blocks it the first time. That is normal.
The steps below are Apple’s way to allow it.


STEP 1 — Try to open the app
----------------------------
  • Double-click:  usbBridgeV3.app

  • A warning appears (Apple could not verify … malware).
  • Click the button:  Done
    (Do NOT click “Move to Trash”.)


STEP 2 — Open Privacy settings
------------------------------
  • Double-click this file in the same folder:

      2-Open-Privacy-Settings.webloc

  • System Settings opens to Privacy & Security.


STEP 3 — Allow the app
----------------------
  • Scroll to the BOTTOM of that settings page.
  • You should see a message that usbBridgeV3 was blocked.
  • Click:  Open Anyway
  • If Mac asks for your password or Touch ID, approve it.
  • If it asks again to open, click:  Open


STEP 4 — Open the app again
---------------------------
  • Double-click:  usbBridgeV3.app  again

  • Look for a small icon in the menu bar (top of screen).

  • Go back to the website (Photo Albums or Notes → USB).
    Status should turn green: “USB Connected & Ready”.


Next time
---------
Just double-click usbBridgeV3.app. You should not need
Steps 1–3 again.

Need help?
----------
If Step 3 does not show “Open Anyway”, complete Step 1
first (double-click the app and click Done), then try
Step 2 again within a few minutes.
